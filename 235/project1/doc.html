Phase 2

Adding Mobile Responsiveness

In-Depth Responsivity Mock-up: View Image

This was a more detailed look at making the more complicated parts of the page mobile responsive and how the layout should adapt to different sized screens. I made three distinct layouts for desktop, tablet (medium screen), and mobile (small screen), and I manipulated the content within each section to accomodate these three screen sizes.

Receiving Feedback

Feedback Document: View Image

After critique day, I attempted to incorporate some of the feedback my classmates had given me. This included increasing font size for smaller screens, darkening the accent color and bolding body text, adding headers for navigation (on medium & smaller screens). Other points of critique I deemed outside of my skillset (adding a toggleable dark mode) or unconstructive (filling in white space that was present in the reference).
